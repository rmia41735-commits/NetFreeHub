IPHONE HELP

Connection guide

▯
Connect to public Wi-Fi

Use these steps when a trusted venue offers Wi-Fi.

  1. 1
    Open Settings

    On your iPhone, open Settings → Wi-Fi.

  2. 2
    Choose the venue network

    Confirm the exact network name with staff. Avoid look-alike networks.

  3. 3
    Complete sign-in if required

    If a captive portal appears, follow the venue's official instructions. Never enter banking passwords into a Wi-Fi sign-in page.

  4. 4
    Disconnect when finished

    Turn off auto-join for public networks you do not use regularly.

♢

A VPN can protect traffic on some networks, but it does not provide internet access by itself or remove mobile data charges.
